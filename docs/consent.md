# Consent

!!! ccard "What is Consent?" {:what-is-consent}
    Consent requires:

    * all participants can consent (age; sober; informed)
    * privacy & no recordings
    * negotiation & safewords

    We use a [FRIES model](https://www.plannedparenthood.org/planned-parenthood-greater-ohio/media/blog-copy-2/would-you-like-fries-with-that-the-5-pillars-of-consent-everyone-should-know) (though go ahead and talk about other models with us, we're dorks, we love that!)

    * **F**reely Given
    * **R**eversible
    * **I**nformed
    * **E**nthusiastic (and while we're here, Explicit!)
    * **S**pecific

!!! ccard "Be a good human" {:be-a-good-human}
    Listen. Be honest. Ask (don't guess). Try things. Say yes. Say no. Respect privacy. Respect people.

    Help build a stronger community. &lt;3

!!! danger h2 "Or Else What?" {:or-else-what}
    You will be ejected from the event, and not be allowed to attend future events. Some consent violations are crimes with scary names, and those have consequences beyond those listed here.

    Please don't make me professionalize around this :(

!!! ccard "A note on mental health" {:a-note-on-mental-health}
    You are responsible for your own mental wellness. We are not doctors; we are not *your* doctors. We are hobbyists and amateurs and dorks, and this is a recreational space.

    If you are currently experiencing schizophrenia, disassociation, severe depression, or otherwise in crisis? You are responsible for your own education and for obtaining your partners' explicit consent to play!
