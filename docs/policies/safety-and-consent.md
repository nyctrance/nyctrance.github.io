# Safety and Consent Guidelines

Hypnosis is a collaborative, vulnerable, and creative practice. The NYC Trance community thrives because our members trust one another to respect personal boundaries and uphold physical and emotional safety at all times.

---

## The Club Consent Model

!!! warning "Ongoing and Enthusiastic Consent"
    Hypnosis requires clear, explicit, and revocable consent. Silence, passivity, or prior consent during a past session does **not** constitute current consent.

* **Explicit Agreement**: Always discuss and establish explicit scope before initiating any trance exercise (e.g., agreed suggestions, touch boundaries, awakening cues).
* **Right to Withdraw**: Any participant may pause, end, or exit a trance or practice session at any moment for any reason.
* **Non-Coercion**: No peer pressure. Observing quietly is always a valid way to participate.

---

## Trance Safety Protocols

Before beginning any induction with a partner:

1. **Physical Stability**: Ensure the subject is seated safely or lying down in an area free from trip hazards or sharp edges.
2. **Emergency Awakening Cue**: Agree upon an unambiguous wake-up phrase or gesture (such as "Wide awake on three" or tapping the subject's shoulder) that will immediately return the subject to full alertness.
3. **Safety Suggestions**: Always include safety fail-safes (e.g., *"If you need to sneeze, adjust your posture, or respond to an unexpected event, you can do so easily while feeling comfortable"*).

---

## Triggers and Boundaries

!!! danger "Off-Limits Suggestions"
    The following categories of suggestions are strictly prohibited at all NYC Trance club events:
    * Suggestions intended to cause pain, humiliation, or non-consensual distress.
    * Post-hypnotic triggers that extend outside the event space without prior explicit written agreement.
    * Any suggestions involving non-consensual financial, legal, or personal obligations.

Always ask your partner about personal sensitivities or known psychological triggers before introducing sensory metaphors or emotional suggestions.

---

## Awakening and Grounding Procedures

Every operator is responsible for properly awakening and grounding their subject at the conclusion of a session.

### Steps for a Thorough Awakening
1. **Remove Temporary Suggestions**: Explicitly clear any temporary phenomena (e.g., stiffness, amnesia, sensory alterations, catalepsy) before waking.
   > *"All suggestions given during this practice session now fade away completely, leaving you feeling refreshed and normal."*
2. **Gradual Numerical Count-Up**: Count up from 1 to 5, pairing each number with increasing physical energy, alertness, and clear perception.
3. **Check Grounding**: Keep the subject seated for at least two minutes after awakening. Offer water, ask simple orientation questions (e.g., *"What day is today?"*), and verify full sensory reorientation before they stand up.

If a subject feels groggy, dizzy, or lingering "trance hangover," notify a Space Steward immediately.
