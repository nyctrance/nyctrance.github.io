# Class Notes & Curriculum

NYC Trance offers regular workshops ranging from foundational trance mechanics for beginners to advanced hypnotic phenomena labs for experienced practitioners.

---

## Foundation Series

The Foundation Series introduces the core cognitive, physiological, and interpersonal principles behind social hypnosis.

### 101: Introduction to Trance Mechanics
* **Focus**: What hypnosis actually feels like, addressing common myths, and demystifying the hypnotic mindset.
* **Topics Covered**:
  * Attentional absorption and focused engagement.
  * The role of expectation, imagination, and cooperation.
  * Simple progressive relaxation and visual fixation inductions.
* **Prerequisites**: Please review **[The Club Consent Model](../policies/safety-and-consent.md#the-club-consent-model)** before attending.

### 102: Rapport, Calibration & Safety
* **Focus**: Developing non-verbal sensitivity and monitoring subject comfort.
* **Topics Covered**:
  * Micro-expressions, breathing patterns, and muscular tension.
  * Testing responsiveness with suggestibility games (magnetic hands, heavy/light arms).
  * **[Trance Safety Protocols](../policies/safety-and-consent.md#trance-safety-protocols)** and pacing suggestions.
  * Comprehensive **[Awakening and Grounding Procedures](../policies/safety-and-consent.md#awakening-and-grounding-procedures)**.

---

## Intermediate Practice Labs

### 201: Fractionation & Deepening
* **Focus**: Moving smoothly into and out of trance states to enhance depth and comfort.
* **Topics Covered**:
  * Fractionation loops (brief awakenings followed by immediate re-induction).
  * Metaphor-based deepeners (descending staircases, elevators, nature imagery).
  * Establishing reliable anchors and relaxation triggers.

### 202: Hypnotic Phenomena & Catalepsy
* **Focus**: Exploring physiological and sensory phenomena in a safe, consensual lab setting.
* **Topics Covered**:
  * Arm catalepsy and eyelid lock.
  * Sensory alterations (temperature, comfort, pleasant heaviness).
  * Ethical boundary setting when practicing motor suggestions.
